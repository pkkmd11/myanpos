# myanpos
Myanmar POS System
A simple Point of Sale system for small business in Myanmar.

Status: Planning

## Features
- Product management
- Sales and checkout
- Inventory management
- Customer management
- Sales reports

## Target Users
- Small grocery stores
- Phone and electronics shops
- Small retail businesses in Myanmar

## Project Goal
MyanPOS aims to provide a simple, affordable, and easy-to-use Point of Sale system for small businesses in Myanmar.
